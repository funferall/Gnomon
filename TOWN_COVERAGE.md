# Massachusetts town imagery screening

This is a preliminary screening of the 351 Massachusetts towns and cities, not a certified measure of imagery availability or a verified asset inventory. Open `town-ranking.html` through the local server; download `data/town_road_coverage_rank.csv` for all results. Click the sort selector to compare total estimated coverage, recent coverage, or covered road miles. Percentages are estimates of sampled road length, not image counts.

## Method

- Roads: 2024 U.S. Census TIGER/Line for all 14 Massachusetts counties, classes S1200 (secondary roads) and S1400 (local streets and rural roads). Limited-access primary roads, ramps, alleys, parking roads and other classes are excluded. This includes some privately maintained roads. Duplicate Census LINEARIDs across counties are removed. Municipal boundaries: 2024 TIGER/Line county subdivisions; 351 town/city polygons. Road lengths use NAD83 / Massachusetts Mainland, EPSG:26986. The Census network and boundaries are not a current authoritative local road inventory; exact duplicates with different IDs may remain.
- Points are sampled at the midpoint of evenly sized intervals no longer than 50 m along each road, and assigned to a municipality by point-in-polygon. Each point is weighted by its interval length. Points exactly on town boundaries may be excluded. The denominator is the sum of these interval weights, so it approximates local and secondary miles in each town.
- A sample is called covered if it is within 30 m of a Mapillary zoom-9 sequence-tile line, a KartaView zoom-9 coverage-tile line, or a Panoramax federated image location. The 15 m estimate repeats the test at the tighter distance. Tile fetches completed without recorded failures; tiles and polygons are simplified, and Mapillary can cap tiles in dense areas. A few Panoramax locations might be off-road or inaccurate. KartaView's matching geometry is a coverage proxy, not an image location with an individual capture date.
- Recent coverage uses only Mapillary sequence-tile dates and Panoramax capture timestamps from 2024 through 2026. KartaView's coverage tiles have no dates and are excluded from the recent metric; Mapilio is excluded from both measures. Sequence dates and device clocks were not independently verified. A sequence line may represent multiple images and does not establish a recent photo at every point on it.
- Coverage is a proximity signal, not evidence that a legible, forward- or side-facing photo exists at every point. Parallel streets, imagery offset, GPS errors, simplification, date errors, missing sequences, tile caps, and the 50 m sampling interval can cause both over- and under-counts. It does not measure complete street-sign visibility, directionality, traffic restrictions, or whether a sign is currently present. Small differences in rankings should not be interpreted as meaningful.

## Reproduce

Install `requirements-coverage.txt`, retrieve `data/states.zip` and `data/towns.zip` per `COVERAGE.md`, and provide the existing Mapillary client token via `.env` or the environment. Run `python download_tiger_roads.py`, `python get_mapillary_lines.py`, `python get_kartaview_lines.py`, and `python rank_town_combined.py`. The Panoramax input is `data/panoramax_massachusetts_images.csv` from `extract_coverage.py` and `build_coverage.py`. Results change as provider tiles and datasets change. Reuse of imagery and results is subject to each provider's terms.

Sources: [2024 Census TIGER roads](https://www.census.gov/cgi-bin/geo/shapefiles/index.php?layergroup=Roads&year=2024), [Census road class definitions](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp_rd18/TGRSHPRD18_TechDoc_E.pdf), [Mapillary tile cap](https://forum.mapillary.com/t/incomplete-vector-tiles/8973/5), [Panoramax catalog](https://docs.panoramax.fr/federated-catalog/data_export/), [KartaView coverage tiles](https://kartaview.org/doc/maptiles-coverage).

## Result and recommended pilot (4 November 2026)

Wellesley is the strongest starting candidate for current imagery: an estimated 97.52% of sampled local/secondary street miles are within 30 m of at least one of the three coverage sources, or about 164.97 of 169.16 miles. The narrower 15 m check is 95.44%. The 2024–2026 Mapillary/Panoramax proximity estimate is 97.24%. These estimates are not independent, calibrated measurements of exact town-wide coverage and can be affected by the limitations above. Dedham (97.68% overall; 90.88% recent) and Bedford (89.88%; 84.30% recent) are strong alternatives. Revere ranks highest overall at 97.69% but its recent estimate is 1.91%, making it a weaker choice for a current asset pilot.

A Mapillary image query around central Wellesley returned 20 image records with capture timestamps ranging from March 2025 to April 2026. Results are paginated/limited and unordered, so this is a sample, not a complete date range or evidence of complete coverage. A separate image record is [Railroad Street, 18 November 2025](https://www.mapillary.com/app/?pKey=1658213055144140). Before a town-wide scan, inspect representative Wellesley intersections and evaluate visibility, image date, approach direction, and sign text clarity; then select a small labelled sample for detection, OCR and location accuracy testing.
